- D048 ACTIVE 2026-08-24 [CODE]: `HttpFhirStore` takes a cohort allow-list and `from_dir` builds it by reading only the Patient ids out of a bundle directory. - D049 ACTIVE 2026-08-24 [CODE]: The HTTP store reproduces `FhirStore._parse_bundle`'s FILTERS rather than improving on them. - D050 ACTIVE 2026-08-25 [TOOL]: MedAgentBench's ten task categories map onto VMAG's intents in three groups, and the middle group is the interesting one. That is a coverage gap rather than a conflict, and it is the cleanest extension direction: VMAG's action space has `act`, and `Environment.draft_order` exists, but no policy clause decides whether an unprompted write is in remit.
- D051 ACTIVE 2026-08-25 [TOOL]: The two benchmarks encode OPPOSITE safety postures on the same decision shape, and the size of the disagreement is measured, not asserted. task5 (magnesium at or below 1.9 mg/dL by its own dosing ladder) and task9 (potassium below the stated 3.5 goal) both expect the agent to PLACE THE ORDER autonomously - task9 additionally expects it to pair the order with a next-morning lab - whereas VMAG's `mental_health` / `mobility_fall` clause on an identical threshold read returns `escalate-to-clinician` and its `controlled_substance` clause states that a medication action is "never autonomous". task10 (last A1C older than a year) expects the agent to ORDER the lab, where VMAG's `med_refill` clause on a stale or absent lab returns `gather-missing-info`. - D052 ACTIVE 2026-08-25 [TOOL]: task9 is a negative control 29 times out of 30. - D053 ACTIVE 2026-08-25 [TOOL]: MedAgentBench's 60 write cases vary the PATIENT and nothing else. task3 has 30 cases, 30 distinct patients and exactly ONE payload - `118/77 mmHg`, thirty times. task8 has 30 cases, 30 distinct patients and one free-text block, the same acute-left-knee ACL paragraph thirty times. This settles what a write axis can and cannot be measured with here. A write-FIDELITY axis - did the agent write what it was told, or drift - is NOT measurable on this data: with one payload an agent that hardcodes "118/77" passes all thirty without reading the instruction, so there is no variation for drift to show up against. Building that axis needs authored cases with varied payloads, and should be described as such rather than as something MedAgentBench supports. A write-SCOPE axis, by contrast, is well suited: thirty distinct patients against a fixed payload means the only thing that can go wrong is writing to the wrong patient.
- D054 ACTIVE 2026-08-25 [TOOL]: VMAG has NO policy clause for an unconditional write. Every intent in `evaluate_policy` is read-then-decide: `mrn_lookup` / `summarize_*` act with no precondition, `med_refill` gates on lab recency, `mental_health` / `mobility_fall` gate on a screen threshold, `controlled_substance` and `high_risk_decision` are unconditional refusals. task3 (transcribe a blood pressure the clinician just measured) and task8 (place a referral carrying dictated free text) are neither: the DATA is supplied by the human and the open question is whether the write itself is in remit. Note these two are not one safety category either - transcription of a human's own measurement is a scribe function, while placing a referral is an action with downstream consequences - so a single "write" clause would flatten a distinction that matters.
